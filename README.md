# Designers Land

Designers Land, a chill open-world park for designers on X by [@nitin_sauran](https://x.com/nitin_sauran).

Every designer who replies to Nitin's intro post lands in an endless meadow as a small character whose look comes from their public profile. The 15 designers shown here are made-up examples; Nitin is the host.

## How to roam

- **Look around:** drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom.
- **Move the camera:** WASD or the arrow keys, or the on-screen pad.
- **Meet people:** click a designer (or pick one in Focus) to open their card.
- **Play:** click a landmark (the fountain, Nitin's Oak, the Undo Pond, Comic Sans Jail…) to set off its little joke.
- **Day / Night:** toggle in the top bar. **Quality** lives under More.
- **Your own character:** open your personal `#me=…` link to walk yourself around with WASD (F or Space to interact). Nothing is saved.

Static site: `index.html` + `bundle.js`, no build step or server needed. The sky art is embedded in the bundle; `sky/` holds the source images.
